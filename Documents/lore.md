## Legend of Milotic
- Most beautiful girl lived in Kaskada
    - She has a habit of taking a bath in the waterfalls of Küstenberg every Tuesday
- Young prince came and fell in love with her
- Would marry him only under the condition, that he may never join her on the bathing
- One day the prince spied on her and discovered, that she was half Pokémon with mermaids tail
- When the girl noticed she was spied on, she turned into a Miltoic and vanished into the depths of Kaskada
- The prince errected Kaskada in the hope to see her again, but she was never to be seen
- Statue of Milotic comemorates her in the city
